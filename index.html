Process

Process

My systematic, tailored process. Scroll to advance through each phase.

01 ⚑ Phase I

Request & Vision

Like all great adventures do, we must first set the scene.

You can do this smoothly and efficiently via the project request. The information you provide really helps me understand whether I'm the right fit for your vision. If I believe I can do the project justice, I'll arrange a call to meet you. I aim to get back to every project request within 48 hours.

02 ⚑ Phase II

Meeting & Scope

Next, we get to know each other properly.

The main aim of this initial call is to establish trust and understanding on both sides. We'll discuss precise goals, timeframes & requirements, clarify any questions and plot out a clear and actionable plan going forwards. My favourite part of these calls is meeting people genuinely excited about what they're building.

03 ⚑ Phase III

Proposal & Timeline

Once a deal is struck, the quest begins.

Once I have a clear understanding of your project scope, I'll put together a proposal tailored to you — whether you have your own brand ideas ready to go, or want help shaping them from scratch. I charge on a per-project basis, rather than by the hour, so you have a clear overview of costs and timeline upfront.

04 ⚑ Phase IV

Design & Construction

Time for the real work to begin.

Once we're both happy with the proposal, I line up the playlist and get to work. Wireframes, design, development — I handle it all, keeping you in the loop every step of the way.

05 ⚑ Phase V

Launch & Handover

Every great adventure must come to an end.

Before launching your site, I put it through a series of checks to make sure it's fast, solid and working exactly as it should. I hand over everything you need to run with it confidently, and I stick around afterwards for any tweaks — launch day should be a celebration, not a panic.
